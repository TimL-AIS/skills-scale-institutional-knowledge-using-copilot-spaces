# Role Onboarding Checklist

## Purpose
Ensure new team members understand their role, responsibilities, and how they interact with other roles across project phases.

---

## General Onboarding (All Roles)

- [ ] Review OctoAcme Project Management Overview
- [ ] Understand the project lifecycle (Initiation → Planning → Execution → Release → Retrospective)
- [ ] Review the Roles and Personas document
- [ ] Understand cross-role interactions relevant to your role
- [ ] Access project repositories and tools (GitHub, project boards, communication channels)
- [ ] Review current project documentation and artifacts
- [ ] Meet with Project Manager for project context
- [ ] Attend team standup and introduce yourself

---

## Role-Specific Onboarding

### Product Owner
- [ ] Review current product backlog and prioritization
- [ ] Meet with Product Manager to align on product vision and strategy
- [ ] Review Definition of Done and acceptance criteria standards
- [ ] Understand stakeholder landscape and communication expectations
- [ ] Review sprint planning and backlog refinement schedules
- [ ] Access product metrics and success criteria dashboards

### Quality Champion
- [ ] Review current test strategy and test plan
- [ ] Understand quality gates and quality standards
- [ ] Review CI/CD pipeline and test automation setup
- [ ] Access quality metrics dashboards
- [ ] Meet with developers to understand test coverage approach
- [ ] Review risk register and current quality risks
- [ ] Understand bug triage and defect management process

### Change Manager
- [ ] Review current change initiatives and status
- [ ] Understand stakeholder landscape and communication channels
- [ ] Review change management processes and templates
- [ ] Access documentation repositories and version control
- [ ] Meet with leadership to understand organizational change priorities
- [ ] Review training materials and onboarding resources
- [ ] Understand change impact assessment methodology

### Agile Coach
- [ ] Review team's current agile practices and maturity
- [ ] Understand sprint cadence and ceremony schedules
- [ ] Review retrospective action items and continuous improvement backlog
- [ ] Meet with Project Manager and Scrum Masters
- [ ] Observe team ceremonies to understand dynamics
- [ ] Review team health metrics and feedback mechanisms
- [ ] Understand impediment escalation process

### UX Designer
- [ ] Review product design system and guidelines
- [ ] Access design tools and asset repositories
- [ ] Review user research findings and personas
- [ ] Meet with Product Owner to understand user story priorities
- [ ] Understand design review and feedback process
- [ ] Review accessibility standards and requirements
- [ ] Understand design-to-development handoff process

### Developer
- [ ] Set up local development environment
- [ ] Review code standards and contribution guidelines
- [ ] Access repository and understand branching strategy
- [ ] Review pull request workflow and review process
- [ ] Understand test coverage requirements
- [ ] Review architecture and technical design documents
- [ ] Pair with experienced developer on first task

### Product Manager
- [ ] Review product roadmap and strategic objectives
- [ ] Access product metrics and analytics
- [ ] Meet with stakeholders and understand their priorities
- [ ] Review user research and customer feedback
- [ ] Understand prioritization framework
- [ ] Review competitive analysis and market research
- [ ] Meet with Product Owners to align on backlog

### Project Manager
- [ ] Review project charter and timeline
- [ ] Understand resource allocation and team structure
- [ ] Access project tracking tools and dashboards
- [ ] Review risk register and mitigation plans
- [ ] Understand stakeholder communication cadence
- [ ] Review project documentation standards
- [ ] Meet with team leads and understand dependencies

---

## First Week Goals

**Target completion: Within first 5 business days**

- [ ] Complete all general onboarding items
- [ ] Complete role-specific onboarding items
- [ ] Attend all relevant ceremonies for your role
- [ ] Establish regular touchpoints with key collaborators
- [ ] Understand current sprint/iteration goals
- [ ] Identify any gaps in knowledge or access
- [ ] Schedule 30-day check-in with manager

---

## 30-Day Check-in

**Schedule for end of first month**

**Success criteria:** New team member feels confident in their role and understands team workflows.

- [ ] Confirm understanding of role responsibilities
- [ ] Review effectiveness of cross-role collaboration
- [ ] Identify any process improvements or documentation gaps
- [ ] Provide feedback on onboarding experience
- [ ] Set goals for next 60 days
