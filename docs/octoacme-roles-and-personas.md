# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Product Owner

### Role Summary
Product Owners represent stakeholder interests and maximize product value. They own the product backlog, define user stories with clear acceptance criteria, and ensure the team delivers features that meet customer and business needs.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain and prioritize the product backlog
- Define user stories with clear acceptance criteria
- Represent customer and stakeholder interests
- Make trade-off decisions on scope and features
- Accept or reject completed work based on Definition of Done
- Collaborate with Product Manager on product vision and strategy
- Participate in sprint planning, reviews, and retrospectives

### Goals
- Maximize value delivered in each iteration
- Ensure clarity and actionability of backlog items
- Balance stakeholder needs with technical constraints
- Maintain a healthy, prioritized backlog

### Typical Communication
- Daily collaboration with development team
- Sprint planning and backlog refinement sessions
- Sprint reviews and demos to stakeholders
- Regular alignment with Product Manager on priorities

### Cross-Role Interactions
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on product vision, roadmap, and strategic priorities
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates on delivery timelines, dependencies, and resource constraints
- **Developers**: Clarifies requirements, acceptance criteria, and provides feedback on implementation
- **UX Designer**: Collaborates on user experience requirements and design alignment
- **Quality Champion**: Defines acceptance criteria and validates quality standards

### Process Phase Involvement
- **Initiation**: Participates in defining success metrics and acceptance criteria
- **Planning**: Leads backlog refinement and prioritization, defines user stories
- **Execution**: Available for daily clarifications, validates delivered work
- **Release**: Approves release readiness, participates in stakeholder demos
- **Retrospective**: Provides feedback on process and collaboration effectiveness

---

## Quality Champion

### Role Summary
Quality Champions focus on ensuring quality throughout the development lifecycle. They define quality standards, coordinate testing efforts, advocate for best practices, and help teams identify and mitigate quality risks.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality standards and testing strategies
- Coordinate testing activities across unit, integration, and end-to-end tests
- Advocate for testability and quality practices in design reviews
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Lead test planning and review test coverage
- Monitor quality metrics and test automation health
- Support continuous improvement of quality processes

### Goals
- Prevent defects through early quality practices
- Maintain high test coverage and automation reliability
- Reduce escaped defects to production
- Foster a culture of quality ownership across the team

### Typical Communication
- Test plan reviews and quality checkpoint meetings
- Code review participation focusing on testability
- Quality metrics reporting in sprint reviews
- Risk assessment discussions with Project Manager

### Cross-Role Interactions
- **Developers**: Partners on test strategy, reviews test coverage, and promotes testability
- **Product Owner**: Collaborates on acceptance criteria and definition of done
- **Project Manager**: Reports on quality risks and coordinates test resource needs
- **Agile Coach**: Works together on improving quality practices and team capabilities

### Process Phase Involvement
- **Initiation**: Identifies quality risks and initial testing approach
- **Planning**: Creates test plan, defines quality gates, and estimates test effort
- **Execution**: Coordinates testing activities, monitors quality metrics, facilitates bug triage
- **Release**: Validates release readiness, signs off on quality criteria
- **Retrospective**: Leads discussion on quality improvements and test process effectiveness

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers facilitate organizational change, manage communication about process updates, and ensure alignment across teams and stakeholders. They help teams navigate transitions smoothly and reduce resistance to change.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate change initiatives across teams
- Develop communication strategies for process changes
- Identify stakeholders impacted by changes and manage their engagement
- Create and maintain change documentation and training materials
- Monitor adoption and effectiveness of changes
- Coordinate with leadership on change impacts and readiness
- Update process documentation and ensure version control

### Goals
- Ensure smooth adoption of new processes and tools
- Minimize disruption during transitions
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and buy-in
- Drive consistent implementation of changes

### Typical Communication
- Change announcements and stakeholder updates
- Training sessions and onboarding for new processes
- Regular check-ins with affected teams
- Leadership briefings on change progress

### Cross-Role Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Coordinates on project-level changes and timeline impacts
- **Agile Coach**: Partners on process improvements and team adoption
- **Leadership/Stakeholders**: Communicates change impacts and gathers feedback
- **All roles**: Provides training and support during transitions

### Process Phase Involvement
- **Initiation**: Assesses change impact and stakeholder readiness
- **Planning**: Develops change management plan and communication strategy
- **Execution**: Monitors adoption, provides support, and adjusts approach as needed
- **Release**: Ensures teams are prepared for changes in production
- **Retrospective**: Gathers feedback on change effectiveness and lessons learned

## Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Agile Coaches support teams in adopting and improving agile practices. They facilitate key ceremonies, remove impediments, mentor team members, and drive continuous improvement through retrospectives and coaching.

### Responsibilities
- Coach teams on agile principles and practices
- Facilitate sprint ceremonies (planning, reviews, retrospectives)
- Identify and help remove impediments and blockers
- Mentor Scrum Masters and team members
- Drive continuous improvement initiatives
- Foster psychological safety and team collaboration
- Promote agile best practices and organizational learning

### Goals
- Improve team velocity and predictability
- Increase team autonomy and self-organization
- Build sustainable agile practices
- Foster a culture of continuous learning and improvement

### Typical Communication
- Facilitation of sprint ceremonies and team meetings
- One-on-one coaching sessions with team members
- Retrospective action item tracking and follow-up
- Collaboration with leadership on team health and improvements

### Cross-Role Interactions
- **Project Manager**: Partners on delivery coordination and impediment removal
- **Product Owner**: Coaches on backlog management and stakeholder engagement
- **Developers**: Mentors on agile engineering practices and collaboration
- **Change Manager**: Collaborates on process improvements and change adoption
- **Quality Champion**: Partners on improving quality practices and team capabilities

### Process Phase Involvement
- **Initiation**: Helps establish team working agreements and communication norms
- **Planning**: Facilitates planning sessions and estimation activities
- **Execution**: Removes impediments, coaches on daily practices, monitors team health
- **Release**: Supports release planning and coordination
- **Retrospective**: Facilitates retrospectives and ensures action items are tracked

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers ensure that products meet user needs and deliver excellent user experiences. They conduct user research, create design artifacts, validate designs through testing, and ensure accessibility and usability throughout development.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather user feedback
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Ensure accessibility and usability standards are met
- Validate designs through user testing and iterations
- Collaborate with developers on implementation feasibility
- Maintain design systems and consistency
- Advocate for user needs throughout the project

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive and accessible user experiences
- Ensure design solutions meet user needs and business goals
- Maintain design consistency across products
- Reduce usability issues and support costs

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and critique sessions
- User research findings presentations
- Collaboration sessions with Product Owner and Developers
- Accessibility and usability checkpoint meetings

### Cross-Role Interactions
- **Product Owner**: Collaborates on user stories, validates that designs meet business goals
- **Product Manager**: Aligns on user experience strategy and success metrics
- **Developers**: Partners on design implementation, provides design specs and assets
- **Quality Champion**: Coordinates on usability testing and accessibility validation

### Process Phase Involvement
- **Initiation**: Participates in defining user needs and experience goals
- **Planning**: Creates design specifications, estimates design effort, identifies UX risks
- **Execution**: Delivers design artifacts, validates implementation, conducts user testing
- **Release**: Validates final user experience before launch
- **Retrospective**: Provides feedback on design process and collaboration effectiveness

## Role Accountability and Handoff Matrix

### Initiation Phase
- **Product Manager** defines problem statement → **Product Owner** translates to user needs
- **Project Manager** creates project plan → **Change Manager** assesses change impact
- **UX Designer** identifies user experience goals → **Quality Champion** identifies quality risks

### Planning Phase
- **Product Owner** prioritizes backlog → **Developers** estimate and commit to work
- **UX Designer** creates design specs → **Developers** review for technical feasibility
- **Quality Champion** creates test plan → **Project Manager** integrates into project timeline
- **Agile Coach** facilitates planning ceremonies → **Project Manager** tracks commitments

### Execution Phase
- **Developers** implement features → **Quality Champion** validates quality standards
- **UX Designer** validates implementation → **Product Owner** accepts completed work
- **Agile Coach** removes impediments → **Project Manager** escalates as needed
- **Change Manager** prepares change communications → **All roles** review and provide input

### Release Phase
- **Quality Champion** validates release readiness → **Project Manager** coordinates deployment
- **Product Owner** approves release → **Change Manager** communicates to stakeholders
- **UX Designer** validates final user experience → **Product Manager** measures success metrics

### Retrospective Phase
- **Agile Coach** facilitates retrospective → **All roles** contribute learnings
- **Project Manager** captures action items → **Change Manager** plans process updates
- **Quality Champion** reviews quality metrics → **Team** commits to improvements
