# OctoAcme Project Management Process

## Overview

OctoAcme employs a structured yet flexible project management approach centered around clear roles, iterative delivery, and continuous improvement. At the core of the process is a lifecycle that moves from project initiation—where ideas are vetted through one-pagers, stakeholder alignment, and risk identification—into planning, where actionable backlogs, milestones, and release plans are developed. This ensures that every undertaking is grounded in business need, measurable goals, and shared understanding among all parties before any work begins.

## Key Personas and Roles

Key personas drive the process:

- **Project Managers** are responsible for orchestrating workflow, keeping schedules and risks transparent, and facilitating meetings
- **Product Managers** define outcomes, prioritize the backlog, and measure impact
- **Developers** build and test features, maintaining technical quality
- **Stakeholders** give input, approve milestones, and ensure organizational alignment

These clear delineations of responsibility foster accountability while supporting a collaborative, cross-functional environment.

## Workflows and Execution

Workflows are managed via project boards and repeatable checklists. Day-to-day execution is fueled by agile practices—daily standups, weekly syncs, and regular demos keep the team aligned on progress, blockers, and upcoming deliverables. Pull request conventions and branching strategies provide a backbone for collaborative development, requiring concise, small PRs with linked issues, documented acceptance criteria, and automated test and linting checks before review. At least one peer review is required before any code is merged, and quality practices are woven throughout the process, from unit and integration testing to manual QA and security scans in CI.

## Communication and Risk Management

Effective communication is a priority, with regular status updates, stakeholder briefings, and risk reviews. Risks and dependencies are captured and tracked in a living risk register, and escalation paths are clearly defined to resolve blockers quickly. Importantly, after each milestone or incident, retrospectives are held to capture learnings, translate them into actionable improvements, and celebrate progress. This commitment to documentation, transparency, and responsiveness makes OctoAcme's process both robust and adaptable, supporting consistent delivery and a strong culture of continuous improvement.
